Delete Your Viper Byte Account

Viper Byte by M.A.D.S. India · Last updated: 28 October 2026

Viper Byte's game needs no account. An account exists only if you opened the private chat: it is an anonymous private ID (for example SNK-7F3K-92QD), with no name, email or phone number. You can ask us to delete it and all server data linked to it.

How to ask

  1. Open the private chat and go to Settings > Profile. Note your private ID (it starts with "SNK-").
  2. Email madsindia.official@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Viper Byte account" and your private ID in the message.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the app, so nobody else can delete your account.
  4. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Never send us your 12 secret words, your PIN or your combinations. We will never ask for them.

What is deleted

  • Your private ID, your public encryption keys and the anonymous sign-in linked to them
  • Your buddy list, blocks and buddy requests
  • Messages still waiting on the server to be delivered
  • Your online status and last-seen time, and your phone's notification address

What is kept

  • Nothing on our server. A record that we handled your request (the date and the private ID) is kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Your messages, names, photos and game data are only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. Copies already on your buddies' phones stay there; we cannot reach them.
